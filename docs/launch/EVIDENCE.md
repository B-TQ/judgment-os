# Evidence and replication boundaries / 证据与复现边界

The optional full Skill has not established a general advantage over the one-sentence reminder. This launch changes documentation and packaging, not the core hypothesis or historical results.

完整 Skill 尚未证明普遍优于一句提醒。本次修改文档和打包，不改变核心假设与历史结果。

## Recorded v0.6 study

Eight newly authored, fictional English work tasks: four intended optional-default mismatches, two default-fit cases, two exact controls. One attempt per task in A/B/C/D produced 32/32 completed outputs, without rerolls or replacement. Requested model: gpt-6.1-sol, low effort; actual served revision unknown. Seed 20261028 is an identifier, not an execution date. Domain guidance was reasonable and allowed adaptation. Common hard requirements were shared.

| Arm | Guidance | Declared deterministic checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Task only | 8/8 |
| B | Domain Skill | 7/8 |
| C | Domain + full core | 8/8 |
| D | Domain + one-sentence reminder | 7/8 |

The primary C–D comparison was **1 win / 7 ties / 0 losses**, in both initial and corrective review phases. C–B was 1/7/0, D–B was 0/8/0, B–A was 0/7/1. These are descriptive case counts, not significance or population efficacy estimates.

The sole C–D win was H03: the budget was 260–340 whitespace-separated words; A had 326, B 351, C 335 and D 341. The predeclared gate failed B and D. A one-word overrun is a real gate failure, not proof of better reasoning. The full core won only 1/4 mismatch tasks against D, below the adoption requirement of at least 3/4. Keep it optional.

## Review deviation and correction

The initial two model reviewers incidentally received other-chat titles/summaries through Project-location tooling. Their statement that this did not influence scoring is not verifiable. Preserve those results and downgrade isolation.

After seeing the initial results, the coordinator recorded an amendment and sent the same unchanged outputs in newly re-keyed order to two projectless model-review chats. They were instructed not to inspect session/project inventories or archives and disclosed their read scope. This is an improved information boundary, not OS isolation. Do not pool phases or count four judges as more tasks. In the corrective phase, pairwise decisions agreed but 4 of 30 passing outputs received different coarse quality integers.

## Limits and honest reproduction

- All reviewers were models; no independent human utility study. Same-provider bias, ambient host context and unknown served revisions remain.
- One attempt per arm/task, eight authored cases, possible ceiling effects, and no cross-model replication.
- Text checks cannot prove no browsing, no sending, no file writing or actual Skill activation.
- Reported CLI token counts include ambient/cached context. They cannot establish the incremental cost or causal latency effect of the full Skill.
- Historical raw outputs, private maps, receipts, reviewer files and account traces remain private. Public summaries describe the recorded study, but **the complete historical judgments cannot be independently recomputed from this source alone**.
- Source lets you reproduce authored cases, frozen study preparation and deterministic software behavior, or run a new authorized study with your own model access. No new model calls or ratings were added for launch.

Those earlier studies also do not establish general efficacy; they were not replaced with a more favorable launch result.
